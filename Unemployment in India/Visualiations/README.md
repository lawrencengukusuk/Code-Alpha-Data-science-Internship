# Unemployment Analysis in India — Visualizations

This folder contains the key plots and visualizations generated for the **Unemployment Analysis in India** project. The visuals support exploratory data analysis and communicate the impact of COVID-19 on India's labour market.

## Included Visualizations

* **Unemployment Trend** — Shows changes in unemployment over time and highlights the COVID-19 period.
* **Employment Trend** — Illustrates changes in estimated employment before and during the pandemic.
* **Labour Force Participation** — Visualizes changes in labour-force participation during the study period.
* **Regional Analysis** — Compares unemployment patterns across Indian regions.
* **Rural vs Urban Analysis** — Highlights differences between rural and urban labour-market conditions.
* **COVID-19 Impact** — Summarizes the measurable changes associated with the pandemic period.

## Purpose

These visualizations provide a clear graphical representation of the dataset, regional differences, and the economic impact of COVID-19 on unemployment and employment in India.
